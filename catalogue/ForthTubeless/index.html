Real-World Performance

Why Ride Tubeless Around the Forth?

From hawthorn hedges along the Dalmeny Estate trails to rough chip-seal lanes across Fife and the Pentlands, inner tubes are the weak link.

Instant Thorn & Glass Sealing

Liquid latex sealant coagulates on contact with air, plugging punctures up to 5mm while your wheel keeps spinning—no roadside tube swaps in Scottish rain.

Lower Rolling Resistance

Eliminating internal friction between butyl inner tubes and the tyre carcass saves watts and allows the casing to deform smoothly over broken tarmac.

Lower PSI Grip & Zero Snakebites

Run 22–38 PSI on gravel/MTB or 55–70 PSI on road without fearing pinch flats on square-edged potholes, tree roots, or cobbles.
